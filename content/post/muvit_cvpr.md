Congratulations to [Albert Dominguez Mantes](/people/albertmantes/) on **MuViT**, a CVPR 2026 Highlight paper with Gioele La Manno and Martin Weigert.

Microscopy images contain information at many scales. MuViT combines observations at different resolutions, helping a model relate fine structures to their wider tissue setting.

Read the [conference paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Mantes_MuViT_Multi-Resolution_Vision_Transformers_for_Learning_Across_Scales_in_Microscopy_CVPR_2026_paper.html) or explore the [open-source code](https://github.com/weigertlab/muvit).
