**Gioele La Manno has been named a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher in 2026**, following his recognition in [2024](/media/highlycited2024/) and [2025](/media/highlycited2025/).

The annual list recognises researchers whose publications have had a significant influence on their fields. In this year's announcement, **Switzerland accounts for 151 Highly Cited Researcher recognitions**.

This recognition reflects the continuing impact of work carried out with students, colleagues, and collaborators. Thank you to everyone who has contributed to this shared effort.
