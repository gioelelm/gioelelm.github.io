We are delighted to celebrate **Gioele La Manno's 2026 Eppendorf & Science Prize for Neurobiology**.

The prize recognises research on brain lipid diversity, including the [Lipid Brain Atlas](/post/lipid_brain_atlas/). This milestone reflects a sustained collaboration across the lab and with Giovanni D'Angelo's group.

Read Gioele's accompanying essay, [“Fats that think?”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ael6217), in *Science*, and the [official Eppendorf announcement](https://www.eppendorf.com/nz-en/company-careers/scientific-awards/eppendorf-science-prize-for-neurobiology/all-prize-winners-finalists/2026-prize-winner-dr-gioele-la-manno/).

Thank you to the students, researchers, and collaborators whose work made this possible.
