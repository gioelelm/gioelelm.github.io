On **15 November 2026**, Gioele received the **Eppendorf & Science Prize for Neurobiology** at the award ceremony in **Washington, D.C.**, during the week of the Society for Neuroscience meeting.

The celebration brought together experts in neuroscience, editors from *Science*, and representatives of Eppendorf. Among those present were **Marina Picciotto**, **Mattia Maroso**, Senior Editor at *Science* and chair of the prize jury, and **Axel Jahns** of Eppendorf. It was a special opportunity to share the lab's work and celebrate with the wider neuroscience community.

The prize recognises Gioele's work on the molecular organisation of brain lipids, including the [Lipid Brain Atlas](/post/lipid_brain_atlas/), developed in collaboration with Giovanni D'Angelo's group. Thank you to everyone in the lab and to our collaborators whose contributions made this achievement possible.
