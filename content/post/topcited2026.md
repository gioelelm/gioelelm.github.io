**Gioele La Manno is included in the 2026 edition of the Stanford/Elsevier top-cited scientists database**, commonly known as the world's top 2% scientists list.

The entry appears in the **single-year citation-impact list**, based on citations received during 2025, with EPFL as the affiliation and Developmental Biology as the primary subfield. This recognition reflects the continuing use of the lab's research by the wider scientific community.

The database was released on 7 October 2026 using an August 2026 Scopus snapshot. It selects researchers through a composite citation indicator: the top 100,000 overall or the top 2% within their subfield. Single-year and career-long impact are reported separately; this announcement concerns the single-year list.
