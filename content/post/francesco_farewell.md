We say goodbye to [Francesco Danese]({{< ref "/people/francescodanese.md" >}}) and thank him for his time in the La Manno Lab.

Francesco [joined us in August 2025]({{< ref "/post/francesco_joins.md" >}}) from Sapienza University of Rome for his master's thesis.

Working with Luca Fusar Bassini, Francesco contributed to our neuroscience knowledge graph project, helping turn information from the scientific literature into structured knowledge that can support biological research. His work brought together machine learning, data science, and the practical challenges of processing a large collection of scientific papers.

Thank you, Francesco, for your contribution and collaboration. We wish you all the best for what comes next and look forward to staying in touch!
