We say goodbye to Lothar Bezzon, who [joined the lab in November 2025]({{< ref "/post/lothar_joins.md" >}}) for his master's thesis research.

Working with Gioele La Manno and Luca Fusar Bassini, Lothar explored computational approaches to spatial lipidomics, including the use of independent datasets to benchmark models and test their predictions.

Thank you, Lothar, for your contribution to the lab. It was a pleasure to share this stage of your research with you, and we wish you all the best for your next steps!
